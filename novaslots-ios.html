Privacy Policy

This policy covers Nova Slots for iOS, the offline casino-style simulation published by the developer associated with RedOctober222333. The game is intended for adults aged 18 and above. All stakes and rewards consist exclusively of virtual coins without monetary value.

Information we collect

The app developer does not collect information from the app. Nova Slots has no user accounts, registration, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, payment processors, tracking, in-app purchases or backend API. Gameplay data and device identifiers are not transmitted to the developer. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Information stored locally

To operate offline, Nova Slots saves your virtual coin balance, stake setting, results and game history, bonus and daily-reward progress, favorites and favorite-reward claim dates, age confirmation and audio preferences in the iOS app's private local storage using UserDefaults. These records remain on your device. The app itself does not sync or upload them; iOS backup behavior depends on your device and Apple backup settings.

Privacy policy and support links

When you choose an external link such as this policy or the support page, Safari or your default browser may connect to GitHub Pages. Your browser and GitHub may handle normal HTTP connection information (including IP address and browser information) under their own policies. This site has no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts operated by us. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay information remains while the app is installed, unless iOS clears the app's data. To remove it, delete Nova Slots from your iPhone. Any backups maintained independently by Apple may be subject to Apple account and device backup settings. The developer does not hold a remote recovery copy of these records.

Age and simulated gambling

Nova Slots is for adults aged 18+. Its age confirmation is a self-declaration and does not collect birth dates or identity documents. There is no real-money wagering, purchase, deposit, withdrawal, exchange for money, or cash prize. Performance in a simulation does not predict success in real-money gambling.
