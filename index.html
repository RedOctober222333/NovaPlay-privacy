NOVA PLAY · ANDROID · UPDATED 27 OCTOBER 2026

Privacy policy

This policy applies to Nova Play, the offline Android game maintained by the developer account RedOctober222333. Nova Play is a simulation for adults aged 18 and over. All coins and rewards are virtual and cannot be redeemed for money or real-world prizes.

Information used by the app

No account, registration or date of birth is requested. The app does not send gameplay data, identifiers or personal information to the developer. It has no advertising, analytics or payment SDKs, and it does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Information stored on your device

Nova Play stores your virtual coin balance, accepted game rounds and bonus progress, favorite games, attached virtual rewards and claim dates, music and sound preferences, reduced-motion preferences, and your 18+ confirmation and onboarding completion. These records support the game's features and are stored in app-private Android storage. They are not synced to the developer or a cloud account by the app. Android backup for these records is disabled.

External privacy link

The privacy-policy button opens this page in your device's external browser. Nova Play does not attach your game progress or identifiers to this link. Your browser and the website hosting provider, GitHub, may process normal connection information such as your IP address and browser details under their own policies. This page contains no analytics, ads, cookies set by us, external fonts or tracking scripts. See GitHub's privacy statement for hosting information.

Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To delete them, use Android Settings → Apps → Nova Play → Storage → Clear storage. Deletion also resets your balance, favorites, rewards and settings; no recovery copy is held by the developer.

Adults only

Nova Play is not intended for anyone under 18. The 18+ screen records a local self-declaration, not identity verification. A user who does not confirm the age requirement cannot enter the game. Coins have no monetary value; there are no deposits, withdrawals or purchases. Success in this simulation does not predict results in real-money gambling.

Privacy inquiries

The developer's privacy contact channel is the Nova Play privacy support page. GitHub account sign-in may be required to send a request. Issues are public: do not post personal, account, health or other sensitive information. You may request a private contact method without disclosing personal details. If you voluntarily contact the developer, your submitted message is used to respond to that request and remains subject to GitHub's service and retention rules. You may ask for its removal through the same channel.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change. The revision date is shown above. The policy for any future advertising, accounts, purchases or online features must be updated before those features are introduced.
